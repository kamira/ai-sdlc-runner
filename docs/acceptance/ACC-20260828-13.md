## Why a note and not seven strikes

Two reasons, and the second is the one that decided it.

**They were true.** Every sentence was accurate on 2026-08-22.

**They are the argument.** *"48 minor versions behind"*, *"five full skill copies"*, *"51 scripts vs
7"* — that is most of the case CHG-20260823-01 acts on when it removes the store. Striking them
would leave the next reader looking at a decision with its reasoning deleted, which is a worse
record than one with a stale paragraph in it.

So the note says the one thing a reader needs — **do not act on any of these** — and the reasoning
stays where it is.

## Why the change records were not touched

`CHG-20260822-01` through `-04` describe vendoring the store. They are **history**: a change record
says what was done at the time, and rewriting it because a later change undid it would make the
ledger a description of the present rather than a record of what happened.

Only the **acceptances** are annotated, because an acceptance is what a reader consults for open
gaps — and an open gap that cannot be acted on is the thing worth flagging.

## Reservations (disclosed, not waived)

1. **Only these three acceptances were swept.** The survey that found them was looking for

   store too.

   not to read, and the distinction rests on the wording being taken as written.
