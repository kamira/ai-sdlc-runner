## Reservations

1. **Both defects are unswept fixes of mine, one from this session.** `CHG-20260903-31` fixed the
   rename slice in `worktree.py` and stopped at the file; `CHG-20260903-27` fixed the fabricated
   verdict in the adjudicators and stopped at the module. Neither seat had to look far. **The
   missing thing was the sweep, not the insight**, and that is why this record ships a rule rather
   than two more fixes.
2. **Criterion 7's rule is a heuristic over eight lines of context.** A reader that parses further
   away slips past it; a line mentioning `.split(` for an unrelated reason is a false positive.
   Narrow on purpose, and the docstring says so rather than calling it a proof.
3. **`frozen_tree.state()` now returns more entries for a rename**, and a caller counting them will
   see one more per rename. Both are things a tree frozen at the commit does not match, so both
   belong — but it is a change in what a verification round is told.
