## The measurement that decides the structural finding

The same revert, on the same code, before and after the fixture was fixed:

| | POSIX | Windows |
|---|---|---|
| before — `Path.write_text` translates | 7 | 5 |
| after — `io.open(newline="")` | 7 | 7 |

`Path.write_text` turned the two rows written with an explicit CRLF into `\r\r\n`, which reads back
as a paragraph break — so on Windows `_PARAGRAPH_BREAK` refused them before the sentence rule was
consulted. Two rounds of corrections were arguing about a number that depended on the platform, and
neither round said so.
