## The two claims that were not true when written

Both dated to commit `513a436` by the `fable` engine:

1. **"Version-checked" routes.** At that commit the routes were **presence**-checked only: a request
   carrying `version: 999` returned 200. Real check-and-advance arrived with CHG-26
   (`Runner.edit` `server.py:362-373`).
2. **"A standing assignment."** At that commit the store governed no run — nothing read it on the
   way to a dispatch. CHG-26 wired it into `cmd_run` and `cmd_serve`; CHG-27 made the registry travel
   with it.

CHG-26's own record documents both. That is the ledger doing its job, and it is why this ACC records
them as *claims corrected by the next change* rather than as defects still open.
