## The reservation: two mechanisms that did not work when shipped

**1. The count that never counted.** This change's test derived its figure as *collected minus
skipped*. `pytest --collect-only` **emits no SKIPPED lines**, so the subtraction had nothing to
subtract, and the code fell through to `... or _KNOWN_SKIPS` — a hard-coded guess that ran on every
machine. Confirmed in that commit's source by the `fable` engine. The mechanism advertised as
"counted, not guessed" was guessing on every run. Its current, correct form — collected against
collected — is CHG-20260823-30's.

**2. The chart nothing read.** The record says *"the chart matches the code"*. At `01004bb` the atlas
carried self-contradictions and a footer naming four pinning tests, **none of which existed**.
`tests/test_schema_atlas.py` was created by CHG-20260823-30. Until then the claim was asserted by
nothing.

Both are the same shape as the round's other findings and worth naming as such: a check that cannot
fail, presented as a check that had passed.
