## How #5 was established, including what the check does not show

A synthetic walk with an effects object attached crashed **inside** `_run_effects`, on
`record_module`, on the empty lap — with an `AttributeError` from the fake's own shape, not from the
engine. That is enough to establish *the engine reached the effect application for that node*, which
is the claim.

It is **not** enough to show what a real effect would do there, and this acceptance does not say it
is. A fake that got as far as the real `effects.run` would answer that; the reproduction stops one
step short, deliberately, because the step it reached is the one the reservation is about.
