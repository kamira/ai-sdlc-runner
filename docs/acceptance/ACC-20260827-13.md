## Why a tool and not a hook

A hook on every commit would fire on ordinary work, and KN-13 is this repository's own record of
what happens next: a red-line check measuring 95% false positives on ordinary briefs is a check
somebody switches off, and switching it off is one flag away. The rule is about **verification
rounds**, so the check belongs where a round starts.

That is a judgement, and it is the reason this change is graded medium while the other fourteen are
low. A reader who thinks the enforcement should be stronger is disagreeing with a choice, not
finding a defect - and the choice is written down here so it can be disagreed with.

## What it does not do

It cannot stop somebody editing the tree **after** the check passes. Nothing short of a lock could,
and a lock on a working tree is a worse cure than the disease. What it does is make the freeze
*recordable*: a round now has a sha to quote, and a reader can tell whether the round's subject was
the commit it claims.

## Not closed by this record

**Nothing enforces that a round actually runs it.** The tool exists and KN-14 names it; a round
that skips it is still possible. Making the run mandatory would mean deciding what a "verification
round" is in code, which is a larger design question than this change.
