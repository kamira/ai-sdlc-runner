## The shape of this round

Two findings were not "this is wrong" but "**your fix made it worse**", and both were right:

- Changing the survey floors to equalities **removed the finding the survey exists to produce**. A
  new module carrying a real violation reported an arithmetic complaint instead, because the count
  assertion ran first.
- `void` — chosen over `wrong` precisely because `wrong` is a general word matched by substring —
  turned out to sit inside *avoid*, *avoided*, *unavoidable*, and unlike `wrong` it now had teeth.

Both were my corrections to the previous round. That is a different failure from shipping a defect:
it is reaching for a fix without measuring what the fix does, in a session whose entire subject is
measuring what things do.
