## Criterion 5 - the one I did not do

The idiom seat's remedy was to move `-25`'s 10-vs-13 measurement into the *Known gaps* entry whose
sentence those numbers quantify. **Attempted, and abandoned.** Reading the two candidate predicates
against `policy.GATES` and `graph.NODES`:

```

rejects_to is not None      15        3     5                 1
```

Twelve and fifteen, under two different node-to-gate mappings, and neither is ten and thirteen.
Three seats reported verifying the original figures across two shas; I cannot reproduce them, and
the record that produced them is withdrawn.

Putting a number into the section whose warrant is *not overstating what is enforced* on the
strength of somebody else's arithmetic is the exact failure this record is about. So the **claim**
goes and the measurement does not move: `-25` no longer says it is kept, and says why.

That is the first finding this session where the seat's remedy was measured and refused rather than
taken.
