## One softness, recorded rather than smoothed over

`test_the_overall_false_stop_rate_is_written_down` tolerates up to **12%** before it fails on its
own. Read alone, that is a weaker guarantee than the change claims.

It does not stand alone: the per-brief tests pin **0 exactly** for each of the forty-six, and
`test_documented_numbers.py` enforces the "0 of 46" figure wherever a document states it. So the 0%
claim is test-backed from two directions. The 12% tolerance is the aggregate test being lenient
about a number the specific tests already nail down — worth knowing, not worth calling a defect.
