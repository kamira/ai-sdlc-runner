## The reservation: the front end is unguarded, and that is the whole change

The two page-content tests that exist are
`test_server.py::test_the_console_has_a_handler_for_every_button_it_draws` and a
`"here.rejects_to" in page` string check (`test_server.py:514-565`). Neither touches the diagram,
the layout code, or the models panel.

The `fable` engine states the consequence directly: **delete the entire `drawFlow` / `drawModels`
chain and the suite stays green.** The `codex` engine reached the same fact and read it as a FAIL.

Recorded as `PASS_WITH_RESERVATIONS` rather than FAIL for one reason, stated so a later reader can
disagree with it: the change's mechanisms are present and correct, and the risk grade the CHG
carries is **low** precisely because the console holds no governance. A front end nothing tests is a
real gap; it is not a governance failure. The gap is named here in full rather than dissolved into
the verdict.
