## Three seats, one conclusion, reached three ways

The defect seat planted the syntax the strip did not know. The risk seat measured the direction of
the failure and found it fails **open** on a partial mis-parse and closed on a total one. The
conformance seat found the disclosure about it false in both halves — the console has carried
`var m = /token=([^&]+)/.exec(location.hash);` since it was written, and the failure a quote inside
a regex causes is a false **pass**, not the false failure the record described.

None of the three could have been reached from the others' evidence. That is the argument for four
seats stated as a measurement rather than as a principle.

## What the retired mutations went to

Two `prose` mutations from `CHG-20260904-13` no longer anchor, and neither guarantee is unpinned:

- *a call handing nothing counts as compliance again* pinned the `any(call.keywords ...)` clause.
  That clause is gone because it had nothing left to do — a call handing nothing cannot satisfy the
  recorded-against-handed rule.
- *the page says `reason` belongs to the tie alone again* pinned one divider's wording. The
  replacement pins the **grouping**, which is what the page was wrong about in both directions.

`test_no_shipped_mutation_has_a_stale_anchor` caught both, which is the guard doing exactly its job:
a stale anchor is not the same as caught.

## Reservations

1. **`_without_scripts_comments` still has no regex mode.** A quote inside a regex still opens a
   string — `/["]/`, `/don't/`. What changed is that the failure is now bounded and **announced**:
   markup is out of scope, and `test_the_scanner_leaves_no_string_open_on_the_real_page` fails if
   the scanner reaches the end still inside one. The shipped `/token=([^&]+)/` carries no quote, so
   nothing is open today. A real regex mode needs to know division from a literal, which needs a
   parser, which is not what this is.
2. **Task 10 makes a bare `pytest` a usage error inside a worktree.** CI installs editable from its
   own checkout and passes; a person who has not set `PYTHONPATH=src` now gets a message rather
   than a wrong answer. It is a behaviour change to the developer workflow and it is stated here
   rather than discovered.
3. **The recorded-against-handed rule reads the constructor keywords.** A method that recorded the
   stop some other way — positionally, or through a helper — would owe nothing and pass. Today all
   three build their record with keywords, and the floor `test_the_rule_looks_at_the_three_methods
   _that_exist` fires when a fourth method appears at all.
