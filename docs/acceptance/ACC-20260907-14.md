## Evidence

### The quotations were stale in their words

```
grep -rn "both are computed" src/   ->  nothing
models.py:419                       ->  "Storing one would let a stale label outlive the truth."
```

Singular. Three documents quoted *"both are computed; storing them would…"*, a sentence the source
stopped carrying when the exclusion moved into `COMPUTED`. Nothing looked, because a fence is not
prose and the italic form is what `_prose` strips as **withdrawn** — the same marker meaning two
opposite things to two rules, with only the attribution to tell them apart.

### An assertion that was never load-bearing

Traced to `599ab8d` (CHG-20260823-22): at that commit clause A was **false** and clause B **true**,
and B's meaning is *"the word `reach` appears somewhere in `save`"*. Later the branches swapped.
Neither form established stripping. Both reviewers accepted the framing — never load-bearing, not
decayed.

**And the evidence that would have vouched for it is a trap**: removing `if k not in COMPUTED`
makes its `split("if k not in")[1]` raise `IndexError`, so a mutation reports `CAUGHT` by
**exception**. Reverting the fix to test the test is fooled here.

### The disagreement, and what settled it

One reviewer wanted a third behavioural persistence test and the deletion of
`test_models_schema.py:61`. The other refused both: two persistence tests already exist and
CHG-20260903-39 argues by name against another copy of one fact; and that line pins the
**dependency** on `COMPUTED` rather than a spelling, so the behavioural tests alone would still
pass if `save` reverted to a literal. Put back with that evidence, the first withdrew both:

> *"No. Do not add a third persistence test."*  …  *"No. Keep `test_models_schema.py:61`. I
> withdraw my deletion recommendation."*

### Prose by subset, a sketch by equality — measured, not assumed

Section 10 names eight backticked identifiers that are not fields — `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`,
`_model_from`, `claude`, `external`, `internal`, `local`, `models`, `modelz`. Equality over prose
would turn a **correct** page red. A fenced key list enumerates, so it gets equality, and that is
where the ten-against-eleven lived.

### Three things the change found in itself

1. The **set-literal rule flagged my own explanation** — my comment reproduced the literal it
   forbids, exactly as its docstring records happening to its first draft. The prose changed, not
   the scope.
2. The **attribution rule refused my own repair**: I wrote that the reason is *"on the line that
   does it"*, and it is on `COMPUTED`'s declaration. Then it refused the fix again, because
   flattening `models.py` left `#:` inside a sentence that spans two comment lines — the same shape
   as `_plain` stripping underscores out of gate names one change ago.
3. The **field rule was still reading fences**. It predates `_prose` by one change, and a repair to
   `API.md`'s sketch made it report a field count inside a JSON block.

### A reviewer's own correction, kept because it is the discipline

It reported measuring `MODELS.md`, `DATABASE.md` and `SCHEMAS.md` as passing, then found it had fed
absolute paths to `is_file()`, which silently filtered them: *"三個文件「全部通過」是空搜尋"*. It
re-ran against the module's own root and reported the real result.
