## The measurement

```
the three shapes          9 / 11 / 13 keys
shared by all three       9
only on a tie             reason · verdicts
only on an incomplete     missing · options · problems · safety
```

under a comment reading *"Every kind of suspension carries the same keys … each says so rather than
omitting the field — a missing key and a false one read the same way only until they do not."*

**Six fields were omitted, not said-so.** The comment's own last clause is the diagnosis.

## And the caller that believed it was mine, yesterday

`CHG-20260904-02` read `stop.get("reason", "nobody has finished it")` at an incomplete stop:

```

what existed           The requirement does not say: the architecture — what the pieces are
                       and which one owns what (asked once). …
```

I copied the tie branch's idiom into a shape whose dict does not carry the key, in the record whose
subject is that *the terminal is the surface nobody swept*. The comment is what made the copy look
safe — which is the whole reason this record fixes the **data** and not the three surfaces.

## Criterion 3 — the ordering was the reason, not an oversight

`intake.stop_reason` was assigned to `halt_reason` **eleven lines after** the shape was built, so
the shape could not carry it whatever anyone intended. Computed before it now, and assigned to both
from one variable, so they cannot drift.

## Criterion 7 — and criterion 5 is what the survivor produced

```
a shape goes back to a dict literal      ##########  red
the shape stops carrying the sentence    ..........  SURVIVED -> ##########  red
an unknown field is passed through       ##########  red
```

The middle survived because the guard asserted the **key was present** — and removing `reason=said`
leaves the key with its empty default. Present is not enough: an empty string is exactly what the
terminal prints. The guard now asserts the sentence names the aspect, names the count, and equals
`report.halt_reason`.

Same distinction as `CHG-20260904-02`'s *"the word was in the output; the line was not"*, one level
down: **the key was in the shape; the sentence was not.** Twice in two days the mutation has found
an assertion measuring the wrong unit, and both times the unit was one step coarser than the claim.

## Reservations

1. **`SUSPENSION_FIELDS` is hand-written**, so a field added to one shape and not to the union is
   refused by criterion 2 rather than silently carried — which is the loud direction, and it is
   still a list somebody maintains. The alternative (deriving the union from the call sites) would
   make the union whatever the sites happen to say, which is the defect this record fixes.
2. **Every suspension grows to 15 keys** on `--json` and the console snapshot. Additive, and the
   six that were absent now read as their empty value — which is what the comment always claimed
   they did.
