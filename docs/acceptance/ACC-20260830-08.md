## The shape of this round

Round 3's finding was that the mutation harness could not tell a mutated tree from an unmutated one.
The fix excused the guard when the record's owner was alive. **That fix is what round 4 found.**

Keying on liveness coupled the guard to `_alive`, and both directions were wrong: a mutation that
broke `_alive` made the guard *fail* (so the tests for `_alive` never ran, and 2 of 11 mutations were
credited to the wrong test), and a reused or unopenable pid made it *skip* (so a genuinely stranded
tree returned rc=0 with `git status` silent, because the record is gitignored).

The second is the worse one and it is mine: CHG-20260830-07 also widened `_alive` so that a denied
`OpenProcess` reads as alive, which is what makes the disarming case reachable at all. I fixed one
defect and, in the same function, made a second one easier to hit — while writing a Risk section
that asserted the opposite.

What replaced it is not a better inference. It is not an inference: the harness sets an environment
variable, and everything that did not come from the harness is a leftover.
