## The reservation: which figures are actually checked

The record says every figure is recomputed. Both engines went looking for the boundary; the `fable`
engine drew it precisely.

**Machine-checked:** node count, gate count, asking-node count, permanent-halt count, seat floor,
roles and seats, work-order field count, backstop ratio, false-stop corpus size and rate, CLI flags,
global-flag ordering, the stated test count (via a `--collect-only` subprocess), the visited-node
count, the schema-atlas link, and the example links.

**Prose only, never machine-checked:** the risk table's semantics, the seats' questions, the "local
only" reasoning, the practice narrative, and **every figure inside `docs/defect-log.md` itself**.

Two consequences, both already borne out by the ledger rather than hypothetical:

- The README's **test count was unchecked when this change was written**. CHG-16 caught it drifting;
  the check only landed in CHG-29.
- `docs/defect-log.md` **is not in the DOCS tuple**, so its own counts are never checked. CHG-16
  found its stated "thirty-two" summing to 35.

A document that says its figures cannot drift, about figures that could, is the exact failure this
repo builds machinery to prevent. Recorded rather than fixed: the sentence is a modification and
needs its own CHG.
