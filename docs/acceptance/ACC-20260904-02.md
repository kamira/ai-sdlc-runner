## The reproduction

```
the stop      node_id=intake_review  gate=None  incomplete=True  undecided=False

the terminal  waiting for:   a decision on None at intake_review
              continue with: --resume --confirm None

the engine    EngineError: confirmed gate 'None' does not exist
```

A run stops in three shapes — a gate, a tie, and a requirement nobody has finished — and `cmd_run`
read two. **The objection is written three lines above, in the branch that gets it right**:
*"Offering `--confirm` here would be offering a control that cannot answer what is being asked."*
It was being offered at the other wrong shape.

`server._require_suspension` was swept for this by CHG-20260903-23 (*"Three shapes, not two"*) and
the console renders all three. The terminal is the surface nobody swept.

## Criterion 6 — and my assertion was passing for the wrong reason

```
the terminal reads two shapes again   ##########  red
it stops saying what is missing       ..........  SURVIVED -> ##########  red
it offers --confirm here after all    ##########  red
```

`assert "architecture" in out` stayed green with the *"waiting for"* line reduced to
*"the requirement"*, because the aspect also appears in the `reason` line the same branch prints.
The word was in the output; the **line** was not. A word-level assertion over a multi-line surface
is the same shape as a name-level count over a renamed field — it measures the wrong unit, and the
mutation is what says so.

## Criterion 5 — the guard's first version was the trap it guards against

It asserted strings against `inspect.getsource(cli)`: a wording check, written into the guard for a
defect that is **about what a person reads**. Replaced with a real `cli.main` run and `capsys`.
Sixth instance of that class corrected in two days, and the first where the guard's subject made
the mismatch obvious on sight.

## Reservations

1. **The three shapes are still enumerated by hand at each surface.** `server`, the console and now
   the terminal each branch on `undecided` / `incomplete` / else. A fourth shape would have to be
   added in three places, and nothing checks that they agree. Naming it rather than building a
   shared reader: the three renderings are genuinely different (a button, a prose line, a flag) and
   a premature abstraction over them would be its own defect.
2. **`report.options` is printed here for the first time on the terminal.** It reached `--json` and
   the console; a long option list will now wrap in a narrow terminal. Stated because it is a
   visible change to output somebody may be reading.
