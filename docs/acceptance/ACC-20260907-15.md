## How the finding was arrived at

Two surveys, and **neither engine reported what the other did**. One ranked a vacuous test
assertion first; the other found that `plan.check` accepts a decision naming a node that does not
exist, and measured the consequence — the run dies after `merge` with the record left open.

Put back to the first with those measurements, it withdrew its ranking. Then a question neither had
been asked — *the walk has no exception path at all, so is that its own finding?* — moved the
winner again: the input defect is one way to provoke a lifecycle failure that **any** exception
causes. The second reviewer accepted the reordering above its own finding, and corrected the
statement of it: *"the closing turn is written on seven of the eight return paths and on no
exceptional path"* is the accurate form.

Both then agreed the two findings are **two records**: cleanup makes failure durable and
intelligible; it does not make an invalid plan acceptable. This is the first.

### The repair a reviewer talked me out of before I wrote it

`try/finally` is the obvious shape and it is wrong here. `conversations.close` is deliberately not
deduplicated — its docstring says why — so a `finally` writes a second closing turn on every normal
run. `test_a_walk_that_finishes_closes_exactly_once` exists so that the wrong repair cannot be
made later without a test saying so.

### The mutations

```
CAUGHT  a walk that dies may leave its record open again
CAUGHT  an interruption may go unrecorded again          (`BaseException` -> `Exception`)
CAUGHT  a crashed walk may be recorded as one that finished again  (`STOPPED` -> `FINISHED`)
```

### A claim I shipped into three places and then withdrew

A reviewer's fourth row — an `EffectError` leaving the record open on a **return** — named the one
`_finish` site of eight that passes no conversation. I changed it, wrote a comment asserting the
consequence, and wrote a test.

**The test passed with the change reverted.** Instrumenting the call sites, every failing-effect
scenario I could drive left the walk at the `after` gate, conversation passed, record closed; the
line I had changed was never reached. The test and its mutation are gone, and the comment now says
what was measured rather than what was expected.

The argument list still ships, on the narrower ground that seven sites pass three arguments and one
does not, and nobody can say why. That is a reason to make them agree. It is not evidence of a
defect, and the record does not claim it is.

## Verdict

**Pass.** A walk that dies, is interrupted, or is stopped by anything the loop does not expect now
closes its durable record with the state that says so — and a walk that finishes still closes it
exactly once.
