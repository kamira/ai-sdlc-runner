## The one thing that was never true

Task 6's done-when says *"KN-1/2/3 marked superseded in body as well as index"*. In
`docs/knowledge/knowledge.md`:

- KN-1 (line 35) carries a `> **Superseded by CHG-20260823-01.**` blockquote. Yes.
- KN-2 (line 58) carries one. Yes.
- **KN-3 (lines 72-84) carries none.** Its INDEX row (line 15) says superseded; its body still
  describes `DashboardModel` and `render_snapshot` in the present tense — and
  `grep -rn "DashboardModel\|render_snapshot" src/ tests/` returns nothing, because
  CHG-20260823-01 deleted them.

Verified as **not later drift**: it was already so at commit `3059308`, the commit that ticked the
task. A reader arriving at KN-3 today is told, in the present tense, how a module that does not
exist behaves.

This is small, and it is exactly the class this repo keeps catching: a done-when ticked on two of
three items. It is recorded here rather than fixed here, because fixing it is a modification and
modifications go through a CHG of their own.
