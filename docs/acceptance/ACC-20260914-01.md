## The finding the registry produced

A fourth mutation was registered first: put `len(report.resumed) < len(report.asks)` back **where
the engine assigns the fact**. It came back `NOT CAUGHT`, and the reason is the sharpest sentence in
this record:

> at that point the option ask has not been appended yet, so the report-wide pair and the
> node-scoped delta give the same answer.

**The defect is the moment, not the expression** — on this graph, and pinned by one entry that
took two attempts to write. The first said the move could not be staged at all. The same comparison is right
before the escalation dispatches and wrong after it, and both callers evaluated it after. A seat
narrowed that: the absence of the option ask is *necessary* for the two forms to agree at the
assignment, and not sufficient — they also agree only while no earlier node has asked anything,
which is the graph property `test_nothing_asks_anybody_before_the_node_the_append_guard_counts_over`
holds. The engine's own form needs neither condition, which is why it is the one that ships. So the repair is not
"stop using a bad expression" but "evaluate it once, where the answer is knowable, and hand it on" —
and the source-side reversion is a *move* — which this record then said a before/after string
cannot stage, and **a seat staged it in two lines** at the suspension's unique anchor. It is the
fourth entry, it is CAUGHT, and it is the only one of the four that pins the moment. The
`NOT CAUGHT` was still worth the group comment it got, because `NOT CAUGHT` and "nothing tests
this" look identical from outside and are not the same fault.

## What is still open

- `test_nothing_asks_anybody_before_the_node_the_append_guard_counts_over` pins a graph property
  that these two callers no longer rest on. The property is still true; its docstring's *reason* is
  now history. Whether anything else rests on it — and therefore whether the test keeps its shape —
  is a question put to the seats with the measurement rather than settled here.
- `NOT_ON_THE_CONSOLE`'s preamble said every entry is *"the record of what governed the run"*. The
  sixteenth is not: it is an input to one decision. The sentence was widened rather than the entry
  bent to fit it.
- Whether the server can be driven to the shape at all. The sweep says no, through a chain about
  work orders and journal keys; this record did not re-run it, and takes it as the prior
  measurement it is. If it is wrong, the server's line was defective too and this record's account
  of it is the understatement rather than the overstatement.
- A narrow `--only <group>` run says nothing about the anchors outside that group, and this change
  broke three of them. `test_no_shipped_mutation_has_a_stale_anchor` is what holds that, and it is
  in `tests/test_mutation_check.py` — a file none of the targeted runs during this build included.
  The habit, not the guard, is what was missing.
