## Why this one mattered more than its count suggests

Eleven of twelve is the best ratio of the four modules measured. The twelfth is the worst kind of
hole, because the wrong answer **looks like the careful one**.

`reach_of` refuses when it cannot find a host. Replace that with `return EXTERNAL` and every test
stays green — and `external` is the strictest grade, the one that makes `validate` demand a key. So
the failure is not "a leak is graded local"; it is that an endpoint resolving to **nothing** is
graded as a real public model, demanded a key, given one, and accepted. Confirmed by construction:
`https://` with a `key_env` is refused with the guard and registered without it.

A mutation to `LOCAL` would have been caught instantly by the existing tests. `EXTERNAL` was not,
because every existing test asks *"is this graded strictly enough"* and none asks *"is this
gradeable at all"*.

## The inputs are ordinary

`https://`, `not a url`, `file:///x`, `://nohost` — all arrive with no hostname. The second is what
somebody types. This is not a crafted-input hole.

## Reservations (disclosed, not waived)

1. **The suffix list is pinned, not checked.** `.local`, `.internal`, `.lan`, `.home.arpa`, and
   "a single label is internal", are judgements about naming conventions. On a network where
   `gpu-box` resolves publicly, a model would be graded `internal` and nothing here would notice.
   The tests hold the rule steady; they do not argue it is right.

2. **`load`, `save` and `_model_from` were not surveyed.** They carry their own refusals — a bad
   envelope, an unknown key, invalid JSON — and only the duplicate-id one is in this group. The
   module was measured through `reach_of` and `validate`, which is where the reach question lives,
   not everywhere the file refuses something.

3. **Four modules measured, seven to go.** `attachments`, `effects`, `intake`, `probes`,
   `settings`, `ship` and `tui` remain unmeasured. The four ratios so far — 7/9, 9/10, 5/8, 11/12 —
   have no pattern worth extrapolating from, which is the argument for measuring each rather than
   sampling.

4. **Verified by the implementer.** The hole was found by the harness; reading `reach_of` did not
   surface it, and the guard sits under a docstring explaining precisely why guessing is wrong.
