## The measured number, and why it is 6 and not 8

The CHG records **8 of 18** and makes a point of it: the author had written "deliberately generous"
in a docstring and guessed twelve, and *"being wrong by four about my own mechanism is the argument
for measuring a safety claim rather than describing it."*

Today the same test asserts **`caught == 6`**. Both engines were asked about this and the `fable`
engine dated it: `git show be5bace:tests/test_flow.py` — this change's own commit — pins `caught ==
8` with the comment `Measured: 8 of 18`. The move to 6 is **CHG-20260823-06's** narrowing of the
word lists, made to bring a 95% false-stop rate on ordinary engineering briefs down to zero, and
pinned there against a 46-brief corpus.

The test's docstring today carries the 8-then-6 history. That is the mechanism this change asked
for working exactly as intended: the number is not an assumption anybody has to trust, it is an
assertion that fails when it moves, and when it moved somebody had to write down why.
