## Evidence

### The guard could not have seen any of it

`assert f"{built} of {built} tables" in catalogue` is a substring-presence check. Pointed at
`DATABASE.md` it **passes today** on line 3 while line 192 says five, and the other guard accepts
the spelling `"N of M tables are built"` — `SCHEMAS.md:368` said *"three of five tables built"*
with no "are", so it matched nothing there either. Re-pointing them was measured and refused before
being proposed.

### `_prose` earns the strictness

Measured on the raw text, the count grammar reports three false positives: a `3 of 5` inside a
blockquote in `DATABASE.md` — kept on purpose, as the record of what `SCHEMAS.md` used to say — a
`CREATE TABLE` inside a fence, and a diagram line in `README.md`. With fenced code, `>` blocks and
the `*"…"*` form removed, **every remaining match is a real claim**. That is what allows the denial
rule to carry no allowlist and no alternative phrasing.

The stripper is therefore load-bearing, so both directions are pinned: a `>` block, the `*"…"*`
form and a code fence are ignored; a live sentence and an **ordinary** inline quotation are not.

### One inventory item that was not a defect, and one that was mine

A reviewer refused `DATABASE.md:192`. Measured: section 2 holds exactly five `CREATE TABLE` blocks
and `halt_routing` is in §0.2, so the sentence is true of its section. A count grammar would have
reported *"says 5, is 6"* — the wrong diagnosis. The repair is the scope, and the qualification
that follows became part of the design: **a bare noun names the global authority only where the
claim does not narrow its own scope.**

I also carried in a claim from the previous round that I had not verified: `SCHEMAS.md:378`'s
`user_version 7`. It is **not** stale — that block is an experiment (*"set all five, close,
reopen"*) and 7 is the value it set to show the pragma persists. It is not touched.
