## Reservations

1. **The console is still checked by reading it, not by running it.** There is no JavaScript
   runtime here, which is why the selection rule was moved into `server.py` where it can be driven.
   What remains unverifiable is that the box *displays* what it reads - the DOM insertion, the
   class, the ordering. Stated rather than hidden: a screenshot test or a JS runtime is a separate
   change, and until then this surface's guarantees stop at the field.
2. **`adjudication_here` duplicates a row already in `adjudications`.** Deliberate: the console
   rendering both is the point - the evidence beside the decision, and the full record below it -
   and a client that wants neither can ignore one key. It does mean two places must agree, and
   nothing asserts that the duplicate is identical to the row it came from.
