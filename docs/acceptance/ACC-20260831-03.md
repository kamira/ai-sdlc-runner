## The measurement that decided veto 1

function:

|---|---|---|
| `{…, "owner": 999999}` | fails | fails |

Four cells in the left column were **green** before this change, and the left column is the CI path.
The harness could not reach them: a mutation run always sets `MUTATION_RUN` to a pid, so
`--only stranded` was 12/12 with the hole open — the one column that does not regress is the only
column the instrument can see.

## What I found by reverting my own fix

The first draft of the tests written to close veto 1 could not tell a failure from a skip. Reverting
each half gave `31 passed, 4 skipped` and `34 passed, 1 skipped` — green, because `pytest.skip`
raises `Skipped` and `pytest.raises(AssertionError)` lets it through. Tests written to close a
silent-disarm defect, disarmed silently, in the same commit.

This is the fourth round in which the instrument, not the code, was the finding. It is the first in
which I found it, and the technique is the one already in the ledger: **revert the fix and require
the test to go red** — not "run the test and see it pass".
