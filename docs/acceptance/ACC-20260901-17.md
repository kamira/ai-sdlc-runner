## Reservations (disclosed, not waived)

1. **Written by the implementer**, on defects somebody else measured — and one of them on a test
   this implementer wrote two changes ago and called a drift guard.

2. **M-3 is the sharper finding and it is mine.** `test_both_ways_to_start_a_run_take_the_same_governance`
   shipped in CHG-20260901-16 as part of a change that found four drift defects, with a docstring
   saying *"the defect is the drift and not the field"* — and it computed one of the two sets. M-1
   was sitting in the other one the whole time. A guard that checks half a symmetric property is a
   guard that reports "no drift" for the half it never looked at.

3. **The journal gains files that are not asks**, and `--ask-journal` is an operator-visible
   directory. `entries()` is the only reader in this repository and it filters by prefix, but a
   script globbing `*.json` and assuming an ask shape will now see `intake-stop-NNN.json`.

4. **The stop is recorded by `cmd_run`, not by the engine.** Deliberate — the engine takes its world
   through `RunConfig` and gives it back through the report — but it means the count is a property
   of *the command*, and any future caller of `engine.walk` that is not `cmd_run` or `serve` will
   silently not count. There are two callers today.

5. **`serve` still keeps its count in `RunState`.** Two mechanisms for one fact, and `serve`'s dies
   with the process — a console restarted mid-intake loses the count where a `run` would not. Not
   unified here because `serve` works and the change is under a design review that touches the same
   surface; it is a real asymmetry and a seat may call it.

6. **M-3′ is verified and left open** — sixteen `RunReport` fields reach no console. It is
   CHG-20260901-15's task 18 and shipping half of it during that design's review would be routing
   around the seats. The count is recorded so the task cannot be scoped from an estimate.

7. **`as_dict()` has no caller in `src/`.** The seat measured it and it qualifies this session's own
   CHG-20260901-16 D4: those six fields became *emittable* and still reach no reader, because
   `snapshot()` builds its own dict and `--json` does not exist. ACC-20260901-16 said both surfaces
   were "documented as open shapes", which is true and reads as more than it was.
