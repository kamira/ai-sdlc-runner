## The reservation: the precision claim is guarded by nothing

Task 4's done-when says the timestamp resolution is *"asserted both ways"*. The `fable` engine tested
that sentence directly: it reverted `_now()` to `timespec="seconds"` and ran. **147 conversation,
demo and recording tests passed.**

`git show --stat 2661659` — this change's own commit — shows that under `tests/` it touched **only
`tests/test_paths.py`**. So no test for the clock was ever added, and the sentence was never
satisfied.

The forward direction (that milliseconds are actually emitted) is guarded by nothing at all. The
backward direction (that old stamps still parse) is covered incidentally by a test about something
else.

The `codex` engine reached the same conclusion by reading. Two engines, two methods, one answer.

*(The `fable` engine also saw an unrelated order-dependent flake during its reversion run —
`test_the_cli_does_not_send_a_conversations_own_bytes_to_the_terminal`, which passes alone and on
rerun. It attributes this to a sibling verifier mutating the shared worktree mid-run. See the round
record: this round violated KN-14 by design, and the clean-tree re-run afterwards was green at 1316
passed.)*
