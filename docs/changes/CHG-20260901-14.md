- Proposed by: the operator — 「先修那個缺陷 再重寫四份設計」, applied to the two the second review

- Risk: **medium** — when a gate over an assessment fires, and what a resumed run reports
- Autonomy: high

## One: the gate over the assessment was silent about the assessment

`lead_assess` proposes the run's risk grade and carries `feasibility_confirmed` with
`gate_when="after"`, whose note gives the reason: *"the thing a person is being asked to confirm IS
the lead's assessment, and stopping in front of it hands them an empty page."*

But `gate_when` only moved the **stop**. The verdict was resolved at the top of the visit, before
the assessment existed. Measured — a plan declaring `low`, a three-model panel unanimously grading
`high`:

```
plan declared  : low
panel proposed : {'a': 'high', 'b': 'high', 'c': 'high'}
agreed         : high

lead_assess    gate=feasibility_confirmed  resolved at risk=low    -> auto
pm_signoff     gate=before_dispatch        resolved at risk=high   -> halt
```

The gate whose whole purpose is that a person sees this assessment **never fired**. The next node's
gate did, one step later, at the grade the assessment had by then established.

This is the circularity CHG-20260827-17 broke, reached from the other side. That change stopped an
assessment **lowering** the gate standing over its own review; nothing stopped it **raising** one
that had already been resolved.

### The fix, and why it can only raise

A gate consulted `after` re-resolves at the grade now in force — but at the **strictest** of that
and the grade it already held:

```python
after = policy.strictest([verdict["risk"], _grade_in_force(cfg, report, node)])
```

`pm_signoff` carries `settles_risk`, and re-resolving flatly at a settled grade that is *lower*
would hand the reviewed thing the height of its own reviewer — the very thing CHG-20260827-17
exists to prevent. Raised only, never lowered, which is `policy`'s tighten-only spine.

After: `lead_assess  gate=feasibility_confirmed  resolved at risk=high -> halt`, and the run stops
there rather than one node later.

## Two: `report.resumed` counted asks that were re-asked

The CLI prints it as *"answered from the journal, not re-asked"*. The walk appended to it on
`ask_id in already` — journal **membership**. `_ask` reuses an answer only when the order *also*
matches, for the reason its own comment gives: *"Reuse the answer only if the question is the same
one. Anything else is answering the new brief with words said about the old one."*

So a resume with a changed brief re-asked everything and still reported most of it as resumed —
measured by the panel at **16 reported against 17 real dispatches**.

The count is recorded inside `_ask` now, where the decision is made, rather than guessed at by four
call sites that could not see it. The four membership checks are gone.

## Risk & rollback

- **A run whose assessment raises the grade now stops one node earlier**, at the gate written for
  it. That is the fix, and it is a behaviour change for every plan graded lower than its assessment.
- **A run whose assessment *lowers* the grade is unaffected** — the gate keeps the stricter height
  it already had, deliberately.
- **`report.resumed` will read lower on any resume with a changed brief**, which is the correct
  number and a smaller one than the CLI has been printing.
- Rollback: revert. The gate over the assessment goes back to resolving before the assessment, and
  the resume count goes back to counting the journal rather than the reuse.
