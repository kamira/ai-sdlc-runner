## The finding

seven-row mutation table. That group holds **six** of them; the seventh - the `_printable` mutation
- has lived in group `cli` since CHG-47's own commit `2932e38`.

Every row re-ran and reported caught during the acceptance round, with counts byte-identical to the
table. The evidence is real; the command printed under it is incomplete.

## What changes

The record gets the second command, with a note saying which row it covers and why the split
exists. The ACC is corrected **in place with a note**, which is this repo's stated convention for a
record that was actually wrong.
