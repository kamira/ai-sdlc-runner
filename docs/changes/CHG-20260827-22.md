# CHG-20260827-22 — a programme is planned at two levels, and the plans have to agree

- Project: ai-sdlc-runner
- Branch: claude/chg-20260827-22-sub-planning

- Type: one capability flag, one tier, and a node that does not exist yet
- Proposed by: the decision to target a mid-to-large organisation shape
- Risk: **high** — it changes the depth of the dispatch tree, which is a property the role table was
  written to hold fixed.
- Autonomy: high
- Related: CHG-20260827-18 (workstreams), CHG-20260823-01 (the role table)

## What this contradicts, on purpose

`policy.py:79` — `Role("pm", can_spawn=False)`.
`policy.py:84` — engineer *"cannot dispatch further, so **the tree stays two deep**"*.

`can_spawn` is described in that file as the flag carrying real weight, and two-deep is stated as a
property rather than a consequence. This change makes the tree three deep. That is the whole of its
risk and it is why it is a change record and not a patch.

## The shape

A main planner decomposes the instruction into workstreams. Each workstream is planned by a planner
at that scope. Those plans are then **reconciled** before anything is dispatched.

The reconciliation node is the part with no equivalent today. `lead_review` reviews the *change*;
nothing reviews the *plans* against each other. Two planners can pick incompatible interfaces,
error conventions or names, and today the first thing that notices is a failing build several nodes
later — or nothing, and the inconsistency ships.

## The question this change has to answer

Each sub-plan is reviewed by whom? Panelling every sub-plan multiplies the most expensive node in
the flow. Panelling none reproduces CHG-20260827-17's defect once per workstream.

Proposed: **reconcile first, then panel the reconciled whole once.** The panel sees what will
actually be built, and disagreement between plans is a reconciliation failure rather than a vote.

### Not claimed

**That reconciliation catches semantic conflict.** It can compare declared interfaces. Two plans
that agree on a signature and disagree on what it means will pass.

**That three deep is where it stops.** It is where this change stops it. The argument that ended
two-deep is available again at three.
