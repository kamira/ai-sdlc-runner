## The finding

KN-14 says: *freeze the tree before verification, and do not touch it until every seat reports.* It
lives in `README.md:524` and `docs/defect-log.md:49`, and **nothing enforces it**. No hook, no
script, no test checks that a reviewed tree is committed or pinned. CHG-20260823-30's statement that
it did not happen again is a statement, not a check.

The acceptance round of 2026-08-27 then broke it: eleven verifiers against one shared worktree,
several mutating `src/` to prove tests bite, one finding another's uncommitted cut mid-audit. The
tree was restored and the evidence held, but the method was wrong - and the rule that would have
stopped it was the one with no mechanism.

Section 8 of the guideline is explicit: **red lines always halt, and the check is code, not a
paragraph.** This one was a paragraph.

## What changes

A small tool that answers the one question a verification round needs answered before it starts:
**is this tree frozen, and at what commit?** It reports the HEAD sha and refuses when the tree is
dirty, so a round has something to record and something to fail on.

It is deliberately a tool a round runs, not a hook on every commit: the rule is about verification
rounds, and a check that fires on ordinary work is a check people switch off - which is KN-13's
lesson and worth not repeating here.
