## The two that matter most

### The gloss narrowed an unowned requirement into the dead end

`「可以依節點 by pass」` appears seven times across the ledger. **Six carry the operator's phrase
unglossed.** The seventh is `README.md`, which reads it as *"pre-authorise by node"* — and
pre-authorisation is precisely the mechanism two withdrawn records failed to build it with.
`by pass` reads as *skip*; pre-authorising is one way to skip. A section whose warrant is not
overstating had quietly picked the reading that guarantees the next record re-derives the failure.

### The gap entry does not say the thing a reader would act on

```
what a declared relaxing class does, per grade
  standard  low      {auto: 10}                      <- no confirm, no halt, no person
  standard  medium   {auto: 9, halt: 1}
  standard  high     {auto: 3, halt: 6, halt_independent: 1}
  normal    low      {auto: 9, confirm: 1}
```

The entry names `merge@low` and `pr@high` — the right two cells — and never says that **at low risk
a change under a declared relaxing class touches a person at no gate at all.** It is also silent
that `relax` refuses to touch a halt, so seven survive at high risk. As written a reader cannot
tell whether this is a total bypass or a five-cell one. It is both, and the grade is the variable.

## Task 5 - a number three seats verified and I could not

`CHG-20260903-25` says it keeps *"the 10-vs-13 measurement, which three seats verified
independently at two different shas"*. `grep` finds the sentence and not the table: the withdrawal
deleted it in the same commit that wrote the claim, which is a hand-off to nobody in the section
whose job is hand-offs.

The obvious repair was to give it a home in `README.md` § *Known gaps*, where the remedy sentence
is the claim those numbers quantify. **Attempted, and abandoned.** Reading the two candidate
predicates against `policy.GATES` and `graph.NODES` gives:

```
                          auto  confirm  halt  halt_independent
gate_when == "after"        12        3     5                 1
rejects_to is not None      15        3     5                 1
```

Twelve and fifteen, not ten and thirteen, under two different node-to-gate mappings. Three seats
reported verifying the original figures and I cannot reproduce them, and the record that produced
them is withdrawn. Putting a number into the section whose warrant is *not overstating what is
enforced* on the strength of somebody else's arithmetic is the failure this whole record is about.

So the **claim** goes rather than the measurement moving: `-25` stops saying it is kept. If the
figures matter to a later record, that record measures them itself.
