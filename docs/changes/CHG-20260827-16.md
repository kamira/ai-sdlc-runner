## The finding

`docs/defect-log.md` states one group's count twice, and the two spellings disagree:

| where | says |
|---|---|
| the table | `**4** (+1 intended collision, below)` |
| the section heading | `3, plus one intended collision` |

Four-plus-one against three-plus-one. **The totals agree; the bare numbers do not**, because they
are not counting the same thing — one includes the intended collision in its bare number and the
other does not.

Three pieces of evidence point three ways and none of them settles it:

- the closing summary says *"the four suite-caught defects"* → 4
- the document's stated sum of 79 needs the table's 4 → 4
- the section holds four `###` subsections, one of which *is* the intended collision → 3

CHG-20260827-11 added the check that found it, and deliberately did not pick. The user has now
confirmed that decision: the numbers stay as they are.

## What changes

Nothing in `docs/defect-log.md`. The ambiguity becomes **KN-17**, because the useful thing here is
not which of 3 and 4 is right — it is that a count carrying a qualifier is two claims, and a check
can hold only one of them.

This is the same move as CHG-20260827-13 made for KN-14: a rule and the mechanism that enforces it
must name each other, or the next reader finds one and not the other. There the mechanism was a new
tool; here it is the check `test_every_defect_log_group_heading_agrees_with_its_row_in_the_table`
already performs, and what is missing is the entry explaining why it skips a row.
